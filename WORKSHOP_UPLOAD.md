# Workshop Upload Notes

## Suggested Title

Side-View Ant Colony War Aquarium

## Suggested Description

A living side-view ant farm wallpaper where two ant colonies dig tunnels, build underground chambers, gather food, avoid rainwater seep hazards, and clash over contested passages.

The default view is cinematic and UI-free for a clean desktop. In Wallpaper Engine's settings panel you can enable event text or stats, change colony colors, use slow RGB or music-pulse color modes, tune performance, adjust ant counts, and control hazards, particles, glass polish, terrain scale, and audio reactivity.

## Highlights

- Two competing ant colonies with workers, soldiers, queens, food stores, base health, and chamber construction.
- Higher-resolution diggable dirt, worn travel paths, contested tunnel glow, surface grass, parallax darkness, glass glare, condensation, scratches, mineral flecks, and ambient particles.
- Ants visibly carry food crumbs and dirt chunks.
- Rainwater seep hazards and user-created fire hazards.
- Wallpaper Engine audio processing for subtle music-reactive lights, glow, and rain ripples.
- Optional Wallpaper Engine RGB hardware sync when the LED plugin and supported hardware are available.
- Built-in Low CPU/GPU visual quality option for laptops or very high-resolution displays.

## Suggested Tags

Relaxing, Game, Nature, Interactive, Audio Responsive

## Upload Checklist

- Use `preview.jpg` as the Workshop preview.
